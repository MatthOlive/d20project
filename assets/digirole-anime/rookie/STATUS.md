# Rookie artwork review

90 of 175 illustrations are saved, including the reused approved Agumon and its 2006 anime alternate artwork.
85 illustrations remain pending after repeated image-generator connection failures.

Each PNG is a separate illustration for individual review. Generated files have not been integrated into the game.

The complete per-species status, source reference, output path and prompt specification are recorded in `../references/rookie/manifest.json`.

Generation used the built-in image generator, prioritizing TCG identity references and using digimon.net as the fallback. The approved Agumon supplies the consistent 2D anime finish and crimson digital landscape. Results await individual visual approval.
